Animal Crossing Deluxe (PS4)

PS4 · PKG · Free

A standalone app you install on your PS4 to play Animal Crossing. It runs on a fork of DolphinPS4 by iHaiDeeZ with Animal Crossing specific changes: settings trimmed down to what actually matters in this game, and a timezone option so the in-game clock is right, which the original port could not do since it was stuck on UTC.

  1. Install the PKG on your jailbroken PS4. This is the download.
  2. Get your own copy of Animal Crossing for the GameCube. No game is included.
  3. Dump it to an ISO or RVZ.
  4. Patch your own dump with Animal Crossing Deluxe by cuyler36. The app does not do this for you.
  5. Point the app at your patched dump and set your timezone.

Before you download

These downloads are meant for people who already own the games they are based on, so please make sure you do. The PKG link is a placeholder until a finished build is ready. Animal Crossing Deluxe is cuyler36’s mod and the emulator underneath is iHaiDeeZ’s DolphinPS4. This port is my own fork of that, with Animal Crossing specific changes on top.
